# ADR 0018–0025: steps

ADR 0018–0025 were accepted by the owner on 2026-11-17 (every question answered, all proposals
approved). Each ADR's §4 lists its steps; each step becomes one or more tasks here, written as a
chain when the step before it is accepted (`docs/tasks/README.md`, "Writing a task"). A step is
"done" only after the owner's hand check where its ADR asks for one; until then its row says
"awaiting the owner's hand check".

**Order (owner, 2026-11-17):** by what a job needs most. ADR 0020 comes before ADR 0021, since
renaming takes the capture time by ADR 0020 (N1). ADR 0017's own rest (U8, U10b, U13, U12d) is in
[`adr-0017-interface-steps.md`](adr-0017-interface-steps.md); the owner places it against this
list.

**ADR 0026** (a folder that follows changes on disk) was accepted by the owner on 2026-11-18 and
placed here after ADR 0024 O1 (F-Q12), as order 2a. **ADR 0027** (display adjustments for
assessment, accepted 2026-11-18) was placed here last, after ADR 0025, by the owner on
2026-11-18; its steps keep ADR 0027 §5's order (A0, A1, A2, A3, A5, A4, A6, A7, A8, A9).
