# ADR 0018–0031: steps

ADR 0018–0025 were accepted by the owner on 2026-11-17 (every question answered, all proposals
approved). Each ADR's §4 lists its steps; each step becomes one or more tasks here, written as a
chain when the step before it is accepted (`docs/tasks/README.md`, "Writing a task"). A step is
"done" only after the owner's hand check where its ADR asks for one; until then its row says
"awaiting the owner's hand check".
