# ADR 0018–0031: steps

ADR 0018–0025 were accepted by the owner on 2026-11-17 (every question answered, all proposals
approved). Each ADR's §4 lists its steps; each step becomes one or more tasks here, written as a
chain when the step before it is accepted (`docs/tasks/README.md`, "Writing a task"). A step is
"done" only after the owner's hand check where its ADR asks for one; until then its row says
"awaiting the owner's hand check".

**Order (owner, 2026-11-17):** by what a job needs most. ADR 0020 comes before ADR 0021, since
renaming takes the capture time by ADR 0020 (N1). ADR 0017's own rest (U8, U10b, U13, U12d) is in
[`adr-0017-interface-steps.md`](adr-0017-interface-steps.md); the owner places it against this
list.

**ADR 0026** (a folder that follows changes on disk) was accepted by the owner on 2026-11-18 and
placed here after ADR 0024 O1 (F-Q12), as order 2a. **ADR 0027** (display adjustments for
assessment, accepted 2026-11-18) was placed here last, after ADR 0025, by the owner on
2026-11-18; its steps keep ADR 0027 §5's order (W0, W1, W2, W3, W5, W4, W6, W7, W8, W9), except W0 (the
region render on RAF), which the owner moved forward the same day as order 1b, before ADR 0024.

**Moved by the owner on 2026-11-19:** ADR 0019 (E1–E3: "Open in {name}" with Ctrl+E, and "Show in
Explorer") as order 1c, after W0 (T197) and before ADR 0024. **The owner, 2026-11-19:** ADR 0019's
addition 1 (E4, the right-click menu on images) is done first, before ADR 0024 O1.

**ADR 0029** (editor profiles) and **ADR 0030** (a catalog across folders) were accepted by the
owner on 2026-11-19 and placed here last, after ADR 0027, as orders 10 and 11 (P-Q and K-Q12: after
the work under way); the owner expects to move them as the work goes on. Their done steps (ADR 0029
P0 and P0b, ADR 0030 K0) are not listed. ADR 0030 K5 also waits for ADR 0022 S1–S4.

**ADR 0031** (releases, version history and installation) was accepted by the owner on
2026-11-20 and placed here by the owner the same day as order 1d, before ADR 0024 O1, so the
first release `0.1.0` (RL7) comes first. RL8 (signing, the owner's) and RL9 (the MSI) follow
later; O1 does not wait for them.
